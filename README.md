In a changing economy with inflation and rising prices, consumers have become more price-sensitive, especially with products like cosmetics. General promotion strategies, such as “10% off for everyone,” are no longer effective and also reduce profit margins.

Traditional systems fail because:

Lack of deep data: They only store transaction history and do not understand customer behavior or preferences.

No personalization: They offer the same promotions to everyone.

Fragmented experience: Customer data is separated across channels (stores, websites).

Our solution is a smart system that works like a small customer data platform (CDP). It can unify data and automatically deliver highly personalized rewards.
